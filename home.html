SMALL HABITS, BIG RESULTS

⚡ Quick Tips

Simple technology habits that can improve your productivity and digital experience.

💾 Back Up Important Files

Keep copies of important school files in cloud storage or another secure location.

🔄 Update Your Apps

Install updates from trusted sources to receive improvements and security fixes.

🔑 Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for important accounts and enable two-factor authentication when available.

🧹 Clean Unnecessary Files

Remove unwanted files and unused apps to free up storage space on your device.

STAY SAFE AND INFORMED

🔐 Technology Safety

Protect your information by following these essential online safety practices.

Protect Yourself Online

  • Never share your passwords or verification codes.
  • Think carefully before opening unfamiliar links.
  • Verify a website before entering personal information.
  • Avoid downloading files from suspicious sources.
  • Limit the personal information you share online.
  • Use privacy settings and enable extra account protection.
