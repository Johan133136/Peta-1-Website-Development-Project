AI Tips for Students

Use AI as a tool that supports your thinking and learning.

Tip 01

🤖 Use AI as a Learning Assistant

AI can explain difficult concepts, provide examples and help you understand lessons.

Good approach:
Ask AI to explain a topic in simpler words, then study and explain the concept yourself.
Tip 02

🧠 Do Not Blindly Trust AI

AI can sometimes provide incorrect or outdated information. Important information should be checked using reliable sources.

Tip 03

✍️ Improve Your Ideas

You can use AI to brainstorm ideas, organize information or improve a draft while keeping your own judgment involved.

Tip 04

🔒 Protect Private Information

Avoid entering passwords, private documents or sensitive personal information into AI tools.

Tip 05

🎓 Learn Instead of Just Copying

The best use of AI for school is to understand the lesson instead of simply copying an answer.

Remember:
Use technology to improve your skills, not replace your effort.
