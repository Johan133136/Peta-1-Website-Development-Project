🌐 Internet Tips & Tricks

Learn safer and smarter ways to browse, search for information and protect your online accounts.

Internet Safety & Smart Browsing

Protect your information and make better decisions online.

Tip 01

🔐 Create Strong Passwords

Use passwords that are difficult for other people to guess. Avoid using easily known personal details.

Better habit:
Use different passwords for important accounts and consider using a reputable password manager.
Tip 02

⚠️ Watch Out for Phishing

Phishing messages may try to trick you into giving away passwords or personal information.

Check first:
Look carefully at the sender, website address and message before clicking a link.
Tip 03

🔎 Check Information Sources

Not everything posted online is accurate. Compare important information with trustworthy sources.

Tip 04

🌐 Check Website Addresses

Before entering sensitive information, make sure you are on the intended website and check the address carefully.

Tip 05

👤 Protect Personal Information

Avoid publicly sharing information such as passwords, private documents and other sensitive account details.
